Teachers / Anthony de Mello

Anthony de Mello

1931-1987 | Indian Jesuit priest

Anthony de Mello was an Indian Jesuit priest and psychotherapist known for his books on spirituality. His teachings blend Eastern and Western spiritual traditions: Christianity, Buddhism and Taoism.

His work emphasizes awareness, awakening from the illusions that cause suffering, and finding happiness through understanding rather than acquisition.
