About Efi Paz

Efi Paz brings together diverse interests: wisdom and awareness, travel photography and the exploration of consciousness.

With a deep interest in Eastern philosophy – Krishnamurti, Alan Watts, Nisargadatta, Atmananda, de Mello and Spinoza – this site is a place for sharing books, recordings, photographs and insights.
