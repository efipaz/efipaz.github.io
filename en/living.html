Living in Peace with Money

A method for examining and changing the way we think, based on the Buddhist path

About the course

Living in Peace with Money is not a course on wonderful ways to make a quick fortune. It is a method for examining and changing the way we think, based on the Buddhist path, used to build a different attitude towards money: thinking, feeling, dealing and touching money become a lesson in understanding our relations with ourselves, with others and with the world.

The course materials are in Hebrew.

Does money bring you calm or unrest? Does it help your relationships with those close to you, or get in their way? Has money really delivered what you expected of it?

The process

Seven parts – from the psychology of money to the integration of money and spirit (Hebrew).

1. The psychology of money 2. Me and money 3. Recognition, description, qualities 4. Liberation 5. Goals and means 6. Money management and investments 7. Integrating worlds
